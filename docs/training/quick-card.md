# CMAI Tracker quick card

Print one side, black and white. Keep it where the phone is.

## Every time you see a behavior

1. Open the app. It opens on **Log**.
2. Tap the behavior. One tap. You are done.

Tap once each time it happens. Two times is two taps.

**Pacing, Restlessness, Mannerisms:** one tap for one stretch. Do not tap again until it stops and starts again.

## If something went wrong

| What happened           | What to do                                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I tapped the wrong one  | Tap **Undo** on the dark bar. It stays for 8 seconds                                           |
| The bar is gone         | Tap **Today**, find the log, tap **Edit**                                                      |
| I logged it late        | Under **When?** on the dark bar, tap **5 min ago** or **15 min ago**. Longer ago: tap **Edit** |
| Not sure which behavior | Hold a tile to read what counts                                                                |

## At the end of your day

Saw none of these behaviors today? Tap **Today**, then **Nothing to log today**. This tells the app the day was quiet, not missed.

## Good to know

- **This app tells no one.** A log is a record. For an injury or an emergency, do what your workplace says.
- Use the code for the person, never a name.
- The logs live on this phone. Keep the app on the Home Screen.
- You cannot break anything by tapping. Every log can be fixed.
