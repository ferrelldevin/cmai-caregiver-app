# Five-minute setup script

For the person who sets up a caregiver's phone. Say the lines in quotes. Do the steps with the caregiver holding the phone, not you. Hand over the [quick card](quick-card.md) at the end.

The practice (minutes 0 to 5) uses the app's made-up practice patients only. Allow about two more minutes to finish. Fill in the problem number on the quick card before you go.

| Minute | Say                                                                                                                                 | The caregiver does                                                                                   | Check                                                 |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 0      | "This app is a tally. You tap a behavior when you see it. That is the whole job."                                                   | Opens the app's web address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android)                                   | The welcome page shows                                |
| 1      | "First we put it on your Home Screen. Always open it from there. Your logs live on this phone only."                                | iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: menu, then Install app. Then opens it from the icon | The app opens with no browser bar                     |
| 2      | "We will practice on a made-up person."                                                                                             | Taps **Try demo data**. The app loads it and opens **Log**                                           | The top bar says P-DEMO-A                             |
| 2      | "Tap a behavior. Any one."                                                                                                          | Taps three different tiles, one at a time                                                            | A dark bar says what was logged, each time            |
| 3      | "That last one was a mistake. Take it back."                                                                                        | Taps **Undo** on the dark bar                                                                        | The bar goes; **Today** shows two logs                |
| 3      | "Now one you saw five minutes ago."                                                                                                 | Taps a tile, then **5 min ago** under **When?**                                                      | The time on the bar moves back and says "(about)"     |
| 4      | "Pacing, Restlessness and Mannerisms are different. One tap for the whole stretch. Tap again only after it stops and starts again." | Taps **Pacing** once                                                                                 | They can say it back: one stretch, one tap            |
| 4      | "A quiet day counts too. On a day with no logs, **Today** shows a button, **Nothing to log today**. Tap it before you go home."     | Taps **Today** and sees today's practice logs. The button is not there now, because there are logs   | They can say when the button shows and what it is for |
| 5      | "Now we clear the practice person."                                                                                                 | **Settings**, **Demo data**, **Remove demo data**                                                    | Settings shows no demo patients                       |

## After the practice (about two minutes)

1. **Only when the lab has said this phone is cleared for real use:** "This is the code for the person you care for. A code, never a name." **Settings**, type the code the lab gave you under **New patient code**, tap **Add**. The top bar shows the code, Period 1, day 1. If the lab has not said so, stop here; the phone stays empty.
2. "This app tells no one. If someone is hurt, do what you always do."
3. "You cannot break it. Every log can be fixed on **Today**."
4. Write the problem number on the quick card and hand it over. Do not leave a card with the number blank.

## If something does not match

The screen names here are the app's as of 2026-11-21: Log, Today, Two weeks, Settings. If the app shows something else, stop and tell the lab; do not improvise.
