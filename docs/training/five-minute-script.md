# Five-minute setup script

For the person who sets up a caregiver's phone. Say the lines in quotes. Do the steps with the caregiver holding the phone, not you. Hand over the [quick card](quick-card.md) at the end.

Everything in this script uses the app's made-up practice patients. Nothing real is entered.

| Minute | Say                                                                               | The caregiver does                                                                                   | Check                                             |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 0      | "This app is a tally. You tap a behavior when you see it. That is the whole job." | Opens the app's web address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android)                                   | The welcome page shows                            |
| 1      | "First we put it on your Home Screen, so the phone keeps your logs."              | iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: menu, then Install app. Then opens it from the icon | The app opens with no browser bar                 |
| 2      | "We will practice on a made-up person."                                           | Taps **Try demo data**. The app loads it and opens **Log**                                           | The top bar says P-DEMO-A                         |
| 2      | "Tap a behavior. Any one."                                                        | Taps three different tiles, one at a time                                                            | A dark bar says what was logged, each time        |
| 3      | "That last one was a mistake. Take it back."                                      | Taps **Undo** on the dark bar                                                                        | The bar goes; **Today** shows two logs            |
| 3      | "Now one you saw five minutes ago."                                               | Taps a tile, then **5 min ago** under **When?**                                                      | The time on the bar moves back and says "(about)" |
| 4      | "Pacing is different. One tap for the whole stretch, not one per step."           | Taps **Pacing** once                                                                                 | They can say it back: one stretch, one tap        |
| 4      | "A quiet day counts too."                                                         | Taps **Today** and finds **Nothing to log today** (it shows on a day with no logs)                   | They know where it is                             |
| 5      | "Now we clear the practice person."                                               | **Settings**, **Demo data**, **Remove demo data**                                                    | Settings shows no demo patients                   |
| 5      | "This is the code for the person you care for. A code, never a name."             | **Settings**, types the code under **New patient code**, taps **Add**                                | The top bar shows the code. Period 1, day 1       |

## Before you leave

1. "This app tells no one. If someone is hurt, do what you always do."
2. "You cannot break it. Every log can be fixed on **Today**."
3. Write the problem number on the quick card and hand it over.

## If something does not match

The screen names here are the app's as of 2026-11-21: Log, Today, Two weeks, Settings. If the app shows something else, stop and tell the lab; do not improvise.
