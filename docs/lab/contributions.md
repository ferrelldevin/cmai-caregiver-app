## How AI was used

Counted on `main` on 2026-11-21 (`git shortlog -sn`): 544 commits. 431 were authored by the Claude coding agent, 108 carry Devin's name and 5 are Dependabot's. Of the 431, 288 name a model in a commit trailer (Claude Sonnet 169, Claude Opus 85, Claude Fable 34); the rest name none. These are counts of commits. They do not measure effort, lines, ideas or how much a person checked.

Most of the 108 under Devin's name are merges of pull requests. Some of those merges were made by the Claude agent through Devin's GitHub account under his standing rule that a pull request may merge when its automated checks are green, a different AI model or service has reviewed it, and it can be reverted. Those are reported to him afterwards. So a merge under his name does not always mean he read that pull request first. Which ones he read is not recorded in the repository; **for Devin to state**.

**Which AI saw what.** Claude (Anthropic), the model that wrote the code, works on the whole repository, and the repository contains the CMAI's item names, definitions and anchors (see `NOTICE`). Other services (Codex, below) are held to a narrower rule. Account settings for retention and training, for either provider, are not recorded in the repository; **for Devin to add**. No participant data exists yet, so none has been sent to any service.
